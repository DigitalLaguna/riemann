main_result: "We prove that the Riemann zeta-function ζ(σ + it) has no zeros in the region σ ≥ 1 − 1/(5.573412 log|t|) for |t| ≥ 2. This represents the largest known zero-free region within the critical strip for 3.06 · 10^10 < |t| < exp(10151.5). Our improvements result from determining some favorable trigonometric polynomials having particular properties, and from analyzing the error term in the method of Kadiri."
