             "Broadly speaking, the results here move between four types of estimates: - Zeta function estimates: zero-free regions on the Riemann zeta function, zero density estimates, and other bounds on the zeta function. - Primary estimates: Bounds on the Chebyshev function and the Mobius summatory function. - Secondary estimates: estimates on other number theoretic functions, such as the number of primes in an interval. - Tertiary estimates: estimates on various problems in analytic number theory."
