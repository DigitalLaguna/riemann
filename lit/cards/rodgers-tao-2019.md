main_result: "Newman showed that there exists a finite constant 𝛬 (the de Bruijn–Newman constant) such that the zeros of H_t are all real precisely when t ≥ 𝛬. The Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to the assertion 𝛬 ≤ 0, and Newman conjectured the complementary bound 𝛬 ≥ 0. In this paper, we establish Newman's conjecture." (Abstract, Forum of Mathematics, Pi 8 (2020), e6, DOI 10.1017/fmp.2020.6)
             "The argument proceeds by assuming for contradiction that 𝛬 < 0 and then analyzing the dynamics of zeros of H_t (building on the work of Csordas, Smith and Varga) to obtain increasingly strong control on the zeros of H_t in the range 𝛬 < t ≤ 0, until one establishes that the zeros of H_0 are in local equilibrium ... But this latter claim is inconsistent with the known results about the local distribution of zeros of the Riemann zeta function, such as the pair correlation estimates of Montgomery." (Abstract; ellipsis marks carder omission)
