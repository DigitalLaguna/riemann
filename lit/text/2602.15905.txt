Abstract
We study the Lagarias inequality, an elementary criterion equivalent
to the Riemann Hypothesis. Using a continuous extension of the harmonic
numbers, we show that the sequence
Bn :=

Hn + exp(Hn ) log(Hn )
n

is strictly increasing for n ≥ 1. As a consequence, if the Lagarias inequality has counterexamples, then the least counterexample must be a
superabundant number; equivalently, it suffices to verify the inequality on
the superabundant numbers.
