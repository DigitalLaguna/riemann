Abstract
We prove a new explicit zero-free region for the Riemann zeta-function, drawing substantially on Heath-Brown’s seminal work on Linnik’s constant. Using these ideas we
are able to prove that ζ(σ + it) ̸= 0 whenever t ≥ 3 and σ ≥ 1 − 1/(4.896 log t).
